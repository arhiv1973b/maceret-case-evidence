## LEGAL QUALIFICATION

### Jus Cogens Violations

Under Article 53 Vienna Convention 1969:

> "A treaty is void if, at the time of its conclusion, it conflicts with a peremptory norm of general international law"

The following are recognized jus cogens:

- Prohibition of torture
- Prohibition of genocide
- Prohibition of slavery
- Right to self-determination

### Erga Omnes Obligations

Barcelona Traction (ICJ, 1970):
Obligations of States towards the international community as a whole:

- All States have legal interest in protection
- Violations affect all States
- Any State may invoke responsibility
